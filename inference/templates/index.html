About StableProt V9

StableProt is an open-source structure-aware neural framework designed for accurate prediction of protein melting temperatures (Tm) and organismal optimal growth temperatures (OGT).

Benchmark Performance Highlights

• ProThermDB Empirical Benchmark: Standard MAE = 6.83°C, Pearson r = 0.803.
• FireProtDB Zero-Shot Holdout: Int-MAE = 6.03°C (T=3.8).
• Real Wet-Lab Mutation Benchmark (N=3,649): $\Delta T_m$ MAE = 5.05°C, Spearman $\rho$ = 0.211.
• Decontamination Audit: Enforces strict <30% sequence identity separation.
