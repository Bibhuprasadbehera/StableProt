About StableProt V9

StableProt is an open-source structure-aware neural framework designed for accurate prediction of protein melting temperatures ($T_m$) and organismal optimal growth temperatures ($OGT$). By combining Foldseek 3Di structural tokens with disjoint multi-head projection pathways, StableProt eliminates inter-task gradient competition and mesophilic probability collapse.

Benchmark Performance Highlights

• ProThermDB Empirical Benchmark: Standard MAE = 6.83°C, Pearson $r = 0.803$, Confidence-Adjusted MAE = 1.42°C ($T=3.8$).
• FireProtDB Zero-Shot Holdout: Int-MAE = 6.03°C ($T=3.8$), outperforming legacy baseline predictors on zero-shot extremophiles.
• Decontamination Audit: Enforces strict <30% sequence identity separation to guarantee zero validation leakage.
