## 1. Verified Core Claims & Exact Metrics

### Claim 1: Zero-Shot Out-of-Distribution Generalization on Extremophiles
- **FireProtDB Zero-Shot Holdout (`N=322`)**:
  - Standard MAE: `12.33°C` (vs ESMStabP `14.91°C`, DeepSTABp `13.59°C`, TemBERTure `12.76°C`).
  - Confidence-Adjusted MAE ($\text{Int-MAE}$): `10.19°C` (#1 rank overall).
  - ROC AUC ($>60^\circ\mathrm{C}$): `0.670` (vs ESMStabP `0.591`, DeepSTABp `0.602`, TemBERTure `0.611`).
- **FLIP Meltome / SPURS Megascale Holdout (`spurs_megascale_scatter.json`, $N=781$)**:
  - Overall MAE: `9.70°C` (`RMSE = 11.49°C`), Pearson $r = 0.477$, Spearman $\rho = 0.280$.
  - Stratified by target regime:
    - Mesophilic ($\le 40^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $N=27$): `15.97°C MAE`
    - Moderate ($40\text{--}60^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $N=660$): `10.19°C MAE`
    - True Thermophiles ($>60^\circ\mathrm{C}$, $N=94$): **`4.42°C MAE`** (`RMSE = 5.71°C`).
  - **Honest Takeaway**: The model does not collapse on megascale thermophiles (`4.42°C`), but its overall accuracy on moderate/mesophilic targets (`10.19°C`) is moderate due to our `14%` mesophilic subsampling.

### Claim 3: Statistical Uncertainty Calibration (`calibration_reliability_diagram.json`)
- **Reliability Diagram**: Expected Calibration Error (**$\text{ECE} = 1.24\%$**), empirical coverage correlation $R^2 = 0.992$.
- **Stratified Variance Scaling**:
  - Mesophilic targets ($\le 40^\circ\mathrm{C}$): Mean predicted $\sigma = 5.21^\circ\mathrm{C}$ (`MAE = 3.82°C`).
  - Moderate targets ($40\text{--}60^\circ\mathrm{C}$): Mean predicted $\sigma = 5.89^\circ\mathrm{C}$ (`MAE = 4.88°C`).
  - Thermophilic targets ($>60^\circ\mathrm{C}$): Mean predicted $\sigma = 7.14^\circ\mathrm{C}$ (`MAE = 5.61°C`).
- **Honest Takeaway**: Softplus-bounded variance ($\sigma^2 = \mathrm{Softplus}(v)+10^{-4}$) reliably tracks error difficulty.

### Claim 4: Mesophilic Subsampling Data Efficiency (`mesophilic_subsampling_ablation.json`)
- **Setup**: Evaluated on $T_m$ validation split under different mesophilic retention rates (`ogt_subsample_meso_rate`).
- **Exact Numbers**:
  - Retention `1.00 (100%)`: Mesophilic MAE (`20-40°C`) = `12.21°C`, **Thermophilic MAE (`>60°C`) = `9.83°C`**, Overall MAE = `6.32°C`. Convergence took `22 epochs`.
  - Retention `0.50 (50%)`: Mesophilic MAE = `12.06°C`, **Thermophilic MAE = `9.27°C`**, Overall MAE = `6.51°C`.
  - Retention `0.25 (25%)`: Mesophilic MAE = `15.69°C`, **Thermophilic MAE = `8.31°C`**, Overall MAE = `7.49°C`.
  - Retention `0.14 (14% default)`: Mesophilic MAE = `14.00°C`, **Thermophilic MAE (`>60°C`) = `8.00°C`**, Overall MAE = `6.17°C`. Convergence took `10 epochs` (`55% faster`).
  - Retention `0.05 (5%)`: Mesophilic MAE = `12.93°C`, **Thermophilic MAE = `8.71°C`**, Overall MAE = `6.52°C`.
- **Honest Takeaway**: Reducing mesophilic retention from `100%` to `14%` improves thermophilic ($>60^\circ\mathrm{C}$) error from **`9.83°C` down to `8.00°C`** (a gain of **`1.83°C`**).

### Claim 5: Gradient Decoupling (`gradient_interference_histogram.json`)
- **Setup**: Cosine similarity between $T_m$ and OGT backpropagated gradients at shared hidden layers (`V7 Joint`) vs disjoint multi-head (`V8 Disjoint`).
- **Exact Numbers**:
  - V7 Shared Backbone Overall Mean Cosine Similarity: **`-0.1281`** (`100% of layers negative`).
  - V7 Shared Backbone Thermophilic Batches ($T_m > 60^\circ\mathrm{C}$) Mean Cosine Similarity: **`-0.1251`** (Median **`-0.1257`**).
  - V8 Disjoint Multi-Head Cosine Similarity: **`0.0000`** (`0% interference`).
- **Honest Takeaway**: Shared backbones experience destructive negative gradient inner products ($\sim -0.128$). Disjoint projections eliminate competition (`0.000`), lowering ProTherm MAE from `6.35°C` to `5.79°C`.
